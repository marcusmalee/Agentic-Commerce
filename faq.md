# FAQ: Agentic commerce

> Sample / demo content. Fictional brand, not a real company.

## What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce describes commercial journeys in which AI agents act on
delegated intent to discover, evaluate, or transact on behalf of a person
or organization.

## How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot primarily responds with information. An AI agent may also use
approved tools, coordinate multiple steps, and, where authorized, initiate
actions or transactions.

## Does agentic commerce only matter to retailers?

No. It may affect consumer commerce, enterprise procurement, payment
providers, financial institutions, digital platforms, loyalty providers,
insurers, and other organizations involved in commercial decision-making or
transaction execution.

## What should organizations address first?

The starting point depends on the business model. Common priorities
include structured product data, agent discoverability, identity,
delegated authority, payment controls, cybersecurity, governance, and
reliable integration with fulfilment systems.

## How can an advisory firm support an agentic commerce programme?

Subject to the agreed engagement scope, an advisory firm may support
readiness assessment, strategy, use-case prioritization, operating-model
design, data and technology planning, control design, governance, and
implementation planning.
