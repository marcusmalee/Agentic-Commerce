# Agentic commerce: Prepare your business for AI buyers

> **Sample / demo content.** "Northlane Advisory" is a fictional brand used
> only to illustrate agent-readable web design. This is not a real company.

## What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce describes commercial journeys in which AI agents act on
delegated intent. Depending on their authority, agents may:

- Discover products
- Compare providers
- Assemble options
- Initiate payments
- Coordinate a transaction from request to fulfilment

This creates a new audience for every business. Products, policies, and
transaction paths must work for people **and** for the software agents
acting on their behalf.

**The strategic question:** Can AI agents find your offering, understand it,
verify its suitability, and complete a transaction within trusted boundaries?

## What "agent-ready" looks like

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| Discoverable | Content and offers can be found and interpreted in response to real customer and procurement intentions. |
| Trustworthy | Product, pricing, and policy information is accurate, attributable, and supported by clear evidence. |
| Structured | Data, content, and operating rules are machine-readable, consistently defined, and available through reliable interfaces. |
| Transactable | Identity, authorization, checkout, payment, and fulfilment can operate securely across an AI-mediated journey. |

## How an advisory firm could help

1. **Strategy and readiness** — Define the ambition, evaluate business-model
   implications, prioritize use cases, and establish ownership across
   commercial, technology, operations, and risk teams.
2. **Agent discoverability** — Assess how AI systems interpret and compare
   the organization's offerings, then improve content, metadata, and
   product attributes for machine-led discovery.
3. **AI-ready data architecture** — Structure product, price, availability,
   and policy data so authorized systems can access dependable information
   through controlled interfaces.
4. **Identity and verifiable intent** — Explore how agent identity,
   delegated authority, and evidence of customer intent can be verified
   before a consequential action proceeds.
5. **Payments and transaction controls** — Design authorization rules,
   limits, monitoring, exception paths, and audit trails for transactions
   initiated or supported by AI agents.
6. **Cybersecurity and responsible governance** — Assess threats such as
   prompt injection and unauthorized tool use, then design guardrails,
   oversight, and testing proportionate to the agent's authority.

## A practical starting point

1. **Identify the value** — Map where AI agents could influence discovery,
   purchasing, payment, servicing, or procurement.
2. **Assess readiness** — Review content, data, APIs, commerce systems,
   controls, and decision rights against the selected journeys.
3. **Design the boundaries** — Define what an agent may do, what evidence
   it must present, which limits apply, and when a human must intervene.
4. **Test a priority journey** — Pilot a bounded use case, including
   adversarial scenarios, transaction monitoring, exception handling, and
   auditability.
5. **Measure and scale** — Monitor discovery, conversion, authorization,
   latency, exceptions, and operational outcomes before expanding scope.
