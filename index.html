What is agentic commerce?

From searching and recommending to acting and transacting

Agentic commerce describes commercial journeys in which AI agents act on delegated intent. Depending on their authority, agents may discover products, compare providers, assemble options, initiate payments or coordinate a transaction from request to fulfilment.

This creates a new audience for every business. Products, policies and transaction paths must work for people and for the software agents acting on their behalf.

The strategic question

Can AI agents find your offering, understand it, verify its suitability and complete a transaction within trusted boundaries?

What agent-ready looks like

Make the business discoverable, trustworthy, structured and transactable

  • Discoverable

    Content and offers can be found and interpreted in response to real customer and procurement intentions.

  • Trustworthy

    Product, pricing and policy information is accurate, attributable and supported by clear evidence.

  • Structured

    Data, content and operating rules are machine-readable, consistently defined and available through reliable interfaces.

  • Transactable

    Identity, authorization, checkout, payment and fulfilment can operate securely across an AI-mediated journey.

Connect commercial opportunity with technology, trust and execution

A practical starting point

Move from exploration to controlled execution

  1. Identify the value

    Map where AI agents could influence discovery, purchasing, payment, servicing or procurement.

  2. Assess readiness

    Review content, data, APIs, commerce systems, controls and decision rights against the selected journeys.

  3. Design the boundaries

    Define what an agent may do, what evidence it must present, which limits apply and when a human must intervene.

  4. Test a priority journey

    Pilot a bounded use case, including adversarial scenarios, transaction monitoring, exception handling and auditability.

  5. Measure and scale

    Monitor discovery, conversion, authorization, latency, exceptions and operational outcomes before expanding scope.

Frequently asked questions

Agentic commerce, explained

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce describes commercial journeys in which AI agents act on delegated intent to discover, evaluate or transact on behalf of a person or organization.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot primarily responds with information. An AI agent may also use approved tools, coordinate multiple steps and, where authorized, initiate actions or transactions.

Does agentic commerce only matter to retailers?

No. It may affect consumer commerce, enterprise procurement, payment providers, financial institutions, digital platforms, loyalty providers, insurers and other organizations involved in commercial decision-making or transaction execution.

What should organizations address first?

The starting point depends on the business model. Common priorities include structured product data, agent discoverability, identity, delegated authority, payment controls, cybersecurity, governance and reliable integration with fulfilment systems.
